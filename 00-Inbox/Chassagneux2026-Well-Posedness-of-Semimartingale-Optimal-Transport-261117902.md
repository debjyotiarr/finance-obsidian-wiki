# Well-Posedness of Semimartingale Optimal Transport with Partial Marginal Constraints for Model Calibration

## Abstract (verbatim from arXiv)
We study the well-posedness and stability of a semimartingale optimal transport problem with partial marginal constraints at several maturities, motivated by the calibration of financial models to finitely many option prices. We connect its probabilistic and Eulerian formulations within the Benamou--Brenier framework. Under convex-duality assumptions, we establish strong duality for every price vector in the extended-value sense and existence of a primal optimizer whenever the value is finite. Under additional PDE assumptions, we establish dual attainment on the relative interior of the admissible price set, together with uniqueness of the primal optimizer. Under further non-degeneracy assumptions, we show that this price set has nonempty interior, on which the dual optimizer is unique and depends locally Lipschitz continuously on the prices. The analysis combines convex methods with regularity and local strong convexity results for Hamilton--Jacobi--Bellman solution maps established in a companion paper. For a fixed deterministic initial state, we also prove continuity of the optimal law on path space in the adapted Wasserstein distance of order two as prices vary within this interior. This stability concerns the optimizer over the full admissible class of semimartingale laws, allowing characteristics that depend on the past. The framework accommodates bounded uniformly continuous payoffs, including European puts in log-price coordinates, without payoff regularization or spatial truncation.
