# Lead-Lag Relationships in Financial Markets: A Comparison of Multiple Clustering Algorithms

## Abstract (summarized — arxiv.org unreachable from this environment; verify at url)
Benchmarks clustering algorithms for detecting lead-lag structure in financial time series against the DTW-KMedoids baseline, on synthetic and real data. Under low noise, alternatives (KShape best) outperform DTW-KMedoids, which also suffers high time complexity and cluster-count sensitivity. Tests a leaders-vs-laggers trading strategy (top 75% by rank as leaders). Submitted 2026-08-25.
