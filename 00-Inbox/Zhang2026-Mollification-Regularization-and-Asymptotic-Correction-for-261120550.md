# Mollification Regularization and Asymptotic Correction for Analytical Expansion of BSDEs with Non-Smooth Terminal Conditions. I. Theory

## Abstract (verbatim from arXiv)
The analytical expansion method for BSDEs of Zhang (2017) breaks down when the terminal condition psi has kinks or discontinuities. We replace psi by psi_epsilon = psi * rho_epsilon and derive a joint error decomposition whose expansion constants explicitly track the derivative blow-up as epsilon tends to 0. For symmetric mollifiers the leading weak bias cancels. The associated balanced rates are conditional on an explicitly stated uniform local-to-global Taylor-propagation assumption: under that assumption the benchmark (l, m, alpha) = (0, 2, 1) gives epsilon* ~ delta_t^{1/3} and weak rate 2/3. Without it, the rigorous additive bound gives no decay when m - 2l = 2; an unconditional positive rate is obtained only for m - 2l > 2. Asymptotic corrections cancel the weak bias through order epsilon^{2J}; their faster exponents are formal targets unless the auxiliary expansion errors and the Picard defect satisfy additional rate conditions, which we state explicitly. For the driver-free fixed-center scheme we identify an n-independent terminal-representation error and show that it depends on the expansion center and on the terminal representative; change-of-basis invariance does not imply centering independence. A Gaussian moment-compression layer combines whitening, a Stein recursion, and knapsack truncation with a jet-conditioned error certificate. This Part I contains the theory, with all proofs self-contained; numerical evidence and implementation measurements are reported separately in the companion paper (Part II).
