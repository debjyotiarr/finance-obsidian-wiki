# Quantum Quantitative Trading: High-Frequency Statistical Arbitrage Algorithm

## Abstract (verbatim from arXiv)
Quantitative trading is an integral part of financial markets with high calculation speed requirements, while no quantum algorithms have been introduced into this field yet.
