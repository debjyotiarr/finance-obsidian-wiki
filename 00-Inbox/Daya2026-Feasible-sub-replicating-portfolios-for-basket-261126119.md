# Feasible sub-replicating portfolios for basket options via the block rearrangement algorithm

## Abstract (verbatim from arXiv)
We construct sub-replicating portfolios of vanilla options for a basket call when the marginal distributions of the underlying assets are known and their dependence is not. To do so, we reduce the n-asset problem to n two-asset problems of the type solved by Hobson, Laurence, and Wang (2005a), and use the Block Rearrangement Algorithm1 (BRA) to approximate the minimising dependence among the assets. The cash component of the portfolio is computed exactly, so that its price is a valid lower bound, and reassembling the BRA output with the original marginals gives an upper bound computed by quadrature; together they bracket the minimum basket-option price. In log-normal examples with three and six assets the bracket has a relative width of at most 0.1%, and the corresponding sub-replicating portfolios are sparse, using only two strikes per asset, while the resulting bounds are tighter than those obtained from a general-purpose multi-marginal optimal-transport algorithm with strikes on a grid. We also give the lower bound and an optimal portfolio in closed form in three special cases: a deep in-the-money basket, a basket with one dominant asset, and jointly mixable marginals.
