# FOMO: FLEX Options and Market Outcomes

## Abstract (summarized — arxiv.org unreachable from this environment; verify at url)
Studies whether single-name FLEX option activity (customizable exchange-traded contracts, centrally cleared) shows up in listed-option markets and underlying equities: 250 trading days (2025-03 to 2026-03), 41 underlyings, 53 event dates. Matched-control diff-in-diff finds a post-event rise in listed open interest (β=0.31, p=0.004); FLEX activity clusters in high-IV, mid-price underlyings; read as listed options hedging/complementing FLEX positions. Also finds an *unsigned* listed gamma proxy does not predict next-day IV — a null that hinges on dealer sign, not evidence against hedging effects. Oct 2026; accepted ICAIF '26.
