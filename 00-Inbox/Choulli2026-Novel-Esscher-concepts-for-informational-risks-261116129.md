# Novel Esscher concepts for informational risks and valuation in market models under random horizon

## Abstract (verbatim from arXiv)
This paper considers the setting constituted by the triplet $(S,\mathbb{F},τ)$. The pair $(S,\mathbb{F})$ is the initial model where $S$ is the discounted price process of risky assets (Stocks) and $\mathbb{F}$ is the ``public" flow of information that is available to all agents over time. $τ$ is an arbitrary random time, which might not be seen through the flow $\mathbb{F}$, and it represents a default time in credit risk and a death time of an insured in life insurance where mortality and/or longevity risks present serious challenges. For simplicity, we suppose $S$ follows a geometric jump-diffusion model with stochastic volatiles and $\mathbb{F}$ is generated by a Brownian motion and Poisson process. In this setting, we address how the Esscher densities and/or measures notions can evolves with the informational risk induced by $τ$. In fact, we introduce the notion of Esscher with degrees-of-freedom associated to the informational risks generated by $τ$. On the one hand, we give various and complete characterizations of these Esscher notions in our informational setting. On the other hand, we fully characterize the Escher-prices bounds, for vulnerable claims (claims built on the occurrence of $τ$), using constrained backward stochastic differential equations (CBSDEs for short hereafter) that appear to be novel as well.
