# Total Positivity Properties of American Options

## Abstract (verbatim from arXiv)
Call option prices, viewed as functions of strike and time-to-expiration, are totally positive of order 2 (TP2) if the ratio of the price of a higher-strike call to a lower-strike call increases with time-to-expiration. A corresponding property applies to put option prices. Prior work has developed conditions for these properties to hold for European options; here we establish these properties for American options. The key condition we require is that log returns on the underlying asset have a log-concave density. Gaussian densities provide an important special case. Within our framework, we show that total positivity properties hold more broadly for American than European options. Empirically, we find that violations of TP2 properties are infrequent, typically small, and reverse quickly. Trades that bet against violations achieve high hit rates. Violations are more frequent among European than American options, a pattern that is qualitatively consistent with our theoretical results.
