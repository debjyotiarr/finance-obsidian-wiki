# Optimal Control in Pairs Trading with Negative-Power Utility

## Abstract (verbatim from arXiv)
We solve a finite-horizon consumption and investment problem in a pairs trading model with an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck spread and utility $U(x)=-x^{-γ}$, $γ>0$. Linearizing the Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman equation gives explicit formulas for the value function and the optimal feedback control. A budget inequality and concavity verify optimality over all admissible strategies with nonnegative wealth on every finite horizon, without Novikov's condition. Under the optimal feedback, wealth and consumption admit deterministic upper bounds when the interest rate $r$ is at most the mean-reversion rate $κ$. If $r>κ$, each exceeds any prescribed level with positive probability at every time after the start. If $r>\sqrt{2+γ} κ$, there is an explicit critical horizon at and beyond which expected terminal wealth is infinite, while optimal expected utility is finite. When the critical time falls before maturity, expected wealth at that time is finite exactly when the initial spread is zero. We also show that the suprema of the negative powers of optimal wealth and consumption are integrable on every finite horizon exactly for orders $0<m\le 1+γ$.
