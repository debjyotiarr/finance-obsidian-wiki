# Stock bubbles in limited participation models with power utilities

## Abstract (verbatim from arXiv)
We prove that the equilibrium state-price deflated cum-dividend stock-price process in Guasoni, Larsen, and Leoni (2025) is a strict local martingale. Our result is based on proving that the unrestricted trader faces an endogenously complete model.
