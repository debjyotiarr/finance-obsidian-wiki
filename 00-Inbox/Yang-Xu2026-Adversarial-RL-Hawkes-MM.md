# Robust Market Making with Hawkes Order Flow and Price Impact via Adversarial RL

## Abstract (summarized — arxiv.org unreachable from this environment; verify at url)
Extends adversarial-RL market making (zero-sum game between maker and environmental adversary over the Avellaneda-Stoikov problem) to an environment with Hawkes self-exciting arrivals and trade-induced price impact, adding an LSTM for the stronger non-stationarity. Concludes adversarial training plus temporal state modeling improves robustness under self-excitation, impact, and regime uncertainty. Submitted 2026-09-19; 18 pp.
