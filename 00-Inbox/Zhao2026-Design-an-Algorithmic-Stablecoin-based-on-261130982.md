# Design an Algorithmic Stablecoin based on a Predictive PID Controller

## Abstract (verbatim from arXiv)
Cryptocurrencies issued based on blockchain technology have attracted increasing attention in recent years. However, their high volatility makes it difficult for them to become circulating currencies. This led to the creation of stablecoins. Existing stablecoins can be divided into three main categories: stablecoins pegged to fiat currencies, stablecoins collateralized by crypto assets, and algorithmically controlled stablecoins. Most existing stablecoins are either of the first two types. We design an algorithmic stablecoin controlled only by a predictive Proportional-Integral-Derivative (PID) controller, which uses the price forecasted by Autoregressive Integrated Moving Average (ARIMA) model as part of its input. Simulation experiments show that, under ideal market conditions, the stablecoin can achieve long-term goals that fluctuate within a pre-defined range. Furthermore, it consistently restores prices to the anchor interval in the presence of exogenous disturbances.
