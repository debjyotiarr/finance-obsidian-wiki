# Gao, Han, Li & Zhou (2018) — Market Intraday Momentum

## One-line summary
The first half-hour return on SPY (measured from the previous close) significantly predicts the last half-hour return in the same direction.

## Key contributions
- Documents the open→close half-hour predictability on SPY, 1993–2013; both statistically and economically significant (market timing works out-of-sample).
- Stronger on high-volatility, high-volume days and around macro news days.
- Proposes slow-moving trading by institutions and late-informed investors as mechanism.

## Data and method
Intraday SPY data; predictive regressions of r_last30 on r_first30 (+ second-to-last half hour); out-of-sample R² and timing-strategy evaluation.
